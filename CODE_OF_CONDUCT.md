# Code of conduct

Everyone interacting in PunchRelay repositories, issue trackers and discussions is expected to be respectful and constructive.

- Be considerate. Assume good intent and disagree without personal attacks.
- Keep discussions on topic and free of harassment, discrimination or hateful language.
- Do not share other people's private information.

Maintainers may edit, remove or lock content that breaks these rules and may block repeat offenders.

Report problems to hello@punchrelay.com.
