## What PunchRelay does

PunchRelay connects a Shopify store to the procurement systems its B2B buyers already use: SAP Ariba, Coupa, Jaggaer, Oracle, Workday and any OCI-capable platform.

- Buyers punch out from their procurement system into a catalog that shows their contract pricing.
- The cart returns to the buyer's system as a cXML or OCI cart for approval.
- Approved purchase orders come back and land in Shopify as regular orders.

Contract pricing is read from Shopify B2B catalogs. Orders are created through the Admin API, so fulfilment, inventory and reporting keep working the way merchants expect.
