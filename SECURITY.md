# Security policy

PunchRelay processes purchase orders and contract pricing on behalf of merchants and their buyers. We take security reports seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **hello@punchrelay.com** with the subject line `Security report`. Please include:

- A description of the issue and its impact.
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept.
- The affected component (app, website, API endpoint) and any relevant URLs.

Do not open a public issue for security problems.

## What to expect

- Acknowledgement within 2 business days.
- An initial assessment and expected fix timeline within 7 days.
- Credit in the release notes if you would like it, once the fix is out.

## Scope

- The PunchRelay Shopify app and its API endpoints.
- The website at punchrelay.com and its subdomains.

Out of scope: Shopify's own platform, third party procurement systems, and denial of service testing. Please do not access or modify data that is not yours.
